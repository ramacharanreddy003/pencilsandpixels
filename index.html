About me

Hi, I'm Rama Charan Reddy Chinnakambaiah Gari Welcome to Pencils & Pixels, a place where my passion for pencil art and photography comes to life. I love turning simple ideas and observations into detailed pencil sketches, and capturing the beauty of nature, places, and memorable moments through photography. Every drawing and photograph tells a story, reflects a moment, or captures something that caught my eye. Through Pencils & Pixels, I’m excited to share my creative journey and the moments that inspire me.
Draw. Capture. Create.
